## About / long description (paste into “About”)

**Kiddotasks** turns household chores into missions kids understand and enjoy — and teaches them that good things are hard-earned.

Parents manage everything from their phone: create chores, set points, approve completions, and watch progress on a clear Today dashboard.

Kids open a shared **Kids Station** with a family PIN — no kid email, no passwords, no bank account.

Why families switch
• Missions, not nagging — daily/weekly routines with stars kids can see  
• No kid accounts — shared family PIN only  
• Parent approvals — chore check-offs and reward claims stay under control  
• Honest points — rewards deduct points only after a parent approves  
• Free plan that stays free — 1 kid · 20 chores · PIN Kids Station included

Try it free → https://kiddotasks-app.web.app

**Tagline:** Missions for kids. Support for parents.

## Voice rules (keep consistent)

- Warm, parent-to-parent; Taglish OK for PH communities
- Lead with **values**: hard work, hard-earned rewards, responsibility
- Always show **parent control** (approvals) — it's a trust signal
- Use proof chips: No kid emails · No bank account · PIN login · Free plan stays free
- No fake testimonials, no invented metrics, no real child names (demo: Maya / Leo / The Santos Family only)
- Always link **https://kiddotasks-app.web.app**
